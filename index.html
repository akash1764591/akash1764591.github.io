Hello! I'm Akash, a recent Electrical Engineering graduate from IIT Patna, currently working on ML Research at LatentForce. My research interests revolve around sequential decision-making problems especially multi-armed bandits and reinforcement learning.

My bachelor thesis (with Dr. Pratik Gajane) on multi-dueling bandits [RLC'26] gives the first best-of-both-worlds algorithms, via a black-box reduction that converts any dueling bandit algorithm into a multi-dueling one.

Prior to this, I interned at IIIT Hyderabad (with Dr. Tejas Bodas) and IRIT, Toulouse (with Dr. Urtzi Ayesta), the latter supported by the Charpak Excellence Scholarship.

Publications
Best-of-Both-Worlds Multi-Dueling Bandits: Unified Algorithms for Stochastic and Adversarial Preferences under Condorcet and Borda Objectives
S Akash, Pratik Gajane, Jawar Singh
Reinforcement Learning Conference (RLC), 2026
ProbML Workshop @ ICML, 2026  (extended abstract)
arXiv / paper / ext. abstract / abstract
Multi-dueling bandits — where a learner selects multiple arms per round and observes only the winner — arise naturally in ranking and recommendation systems. We ask: can a single algorithm perform optimally in both stochastic and adversarial environments, without knowing which regime it faces? We answer affirmatively, giving the first best-of-both-worlds algorithms for multi-dueling bandits under both Condorcet and Borda objectives, with matching lower bounds for the Condorcet setting and near optimal bounds for the Borda setting.

Selected Projects
Fair Multi-objective RL  [code]

Implemented the Fair Multi-Objective DQN (FMDQ) algorithm substituting standard linear scalarization with a Generalized Gini Welfare function. Designed to be fully compatible with the morl-baselines library for benchmarking.

PolicyChain  [code]

Python tool that flattens an MDP and policy into its induced Markov chain. Analyzes structural properties (communicating classes, ergodicity) and generates visualizations like policy graphs and heatmaps.

Smart Product Pricing — Amazon ML Challenge  [code]

Formulated price prediction as a text regression problem. Fine-tuned deberta-v3-large with a custom regression head, engineered a hybrid pipeline with Regex and LLM imputation.

Thompson Sampling for Quantum Entanglement  [report]

Formulated entanglement detection as a multi-armed bandit problem. Used Top-Two Thompson Sampling to select measurements efficiently, identifying entangled states with far fewer samples than traditional tomography.
