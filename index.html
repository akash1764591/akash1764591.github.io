Bio

At LatentForce, I build coding agents that navigate large codebases and coordinate work across models. My broader research interests are learning and decision-making under limited or structured feedback.

My bachelor’s thesis with Pratik Gajane studied best-of-both-worlds multi-dueling bandits. Earlier, I worked on scheduling with Urtzi Ayesta at IRIT Toulouse and Markovian bandits with Tejas Bodas at IIIT Hyderabad. I am grateful to the Charpak Scholarship for supporting my stay in Toulouse.

I’m always happy to exchange ideas or discuss interesting problems—feel free to email me.
