i studied electrical engineering at iit patna. i am interested in systems that have to act without seeing the full picture.

sometimes the missing information is a reward, sometimes it is another player’s preference, and sometimes it is the small piece of a codebase needed to solve a task. during college, this led me to work on reinforcement learning, markovian bandits, and multi-dueling bandits.

my bachelor’s thesis focused on learning algorithms for multi-dueling bandits, built around a simple reduction from multi-way winner feedback to ordinary duels. the problems i work on now look different, but the underlying question remains much the same: how can an agent find the right evidence and make a reliable decision from limited feedback?
