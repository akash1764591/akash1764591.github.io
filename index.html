About

At LatentForce, I work on agents that search large codebases, gather the right evidence, and coordinate across models. My broader research interests are learning and decision-making under limited or structured feedback.

My bachelor’s thesis with Pratik Gajane asked whether one multi-dueling bandit algorithm could handle both stochastic and adversarial preferences. Earlier, I studied scheduling with Urtzi Ayesta at IRIT Toulouse and Markovian bandits with Tejas Bodas at IIIT Hyderabad. The Charpak Scholarship supported my time in Toulouse.

If you’re working on related problems, feel free to mail me.

Research

Preprint
Fairness in Two-Player Zero-Sum Games with Bandit Feedback
S Akash, Pratik Gajane

What does it cost to give every action a fair chance when players observe only bandit feedback? We recast the constrained game and bound both regret and the price of fairness.

Paper
RLC ’26ProbML @ ICML ’26
Best-of-Both-Worlds Multi-Dueling Bandits: Unified Algorithms for Stochastic and Adversarial Preferences under Condorcet and Borda Objectives
S Akash, Pratik Gajane, Jawar Singh

Can one algorithm learn from predictable preferences without becoming fragile to adversarial ones? We answer yes for multi-dueling bandits under both Condorcet and Borda objectives.
